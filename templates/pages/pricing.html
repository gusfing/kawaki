What moves the price

Scope decides it, not the number of pages alone.

Pushes it up

  • Many distinct page templates
  • Interactive tools, calculators or sign-in
  • Integrations: headless Shopify, custom APIs, AI automation
  • Migrating a large existing site

Keeps it down

  • Content and photography ready before design
  • A clear list of must-haves for launch
  • One decision-maker for reviews
  • Later features planned as later milestones
