Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why we collect it, who handles it for us and how to have it changed or deleted.

Last updated
16 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Kawaki Studios LLP, a web development studio in New Delhi, India. In this policy, “we” means Kawaki Studios LLP. For anything about your data, write to hello@kawaki.co.in.

What we collect

When you send us a brief, use the contact form or leave details in the chat, we collect what you give us:

  • your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number
  • the service and budget range you choose, and your message
  • the page you sent it from and the date and time
  • for briefs sent from the chat, the conversation in that chat window

When you browse the site, we use Microsoft Clarity to see how pages are used, such as clicks, scrolling and the pages visited, so we can find and fix problems. Clarity uses cookies for this. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, which include your IP address and browser details.

Some features keep small notes in your own browser: the chat keeps the conversation until you close the tab, and the brief popup remembers that you closed it or sent a brief so it does not appear again. These notes stay on your device.

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal
  • to keep a record of our conversations with you
  • to understand which pages work and fix the ones that do not

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

A few services process data on our behalf: Vercel hosts the website and receives form submissions, Google Sheets stores the record of enquiries, and Microsoft Clarity provides the usage analytics. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also covered by WhatsApp’s own terms. Some older pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when they load.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply, to work with you and to keep proper business records. You can ask us to delete your details earlier, and we will unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent to us using it. Email hello@kawaki.co.in and we will reply within the time the law allows. You can also block cookies in your browser; the website works without them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
