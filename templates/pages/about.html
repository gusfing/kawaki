About the studio

Kawaki Studios LLP is a web engineering studio in New Delhi. We design and build custom websites, web applications and Shopify stores, and fix the ones that break.

How it started

About eight years ago, three of us started learning graphic design, website development and digital marketing, mostly out of curiosity. Years of client work at home and abroad later, that curiosity became Kawaki Studios: a small studio that designs and writes its own code instead of assembling sites from builders and bought themes.
