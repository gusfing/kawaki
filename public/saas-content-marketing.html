What SaaS content marketing covers

SaaS content marketing at Kawaki Studios is run by people who also build websites, so what you publish and how the site converts are planned together. We choose topics from real search and sales questions, write pages that answer them plainly, and fix the forms and pages where interested visitors drop off.

  1. 01

    Planned from demand

    Topics chosen from search data and the questions your sales team hears.

  2. 02

    Written plainly

    Clear answers first, no filler, and facts you can stand behind.

  3. 03

    Connected to enquiries

    Every piece leads to a next step that is easy to take.

Where SaaS content marketing usually goes wrong

Publishing on a schedule without a plan, chasing traffic that never buys and sending visitors to pages that do not convert are the usual problems. SaaS content marketing starts from the questions buyers ask and ends at an enquiry.

  1. 01

    Topic and keyword plan

    Clusters mapped to buyer questions and the pages that answer them.

  2. 02

    Writing and editing

    Articles and landing pages written plainly and checked for accuracy.

  3. 03

    Conversion fixes

    Forms, calls to action and page structure tested and improved.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    Traffic, enquiries and the pages that produced them, monthly.

How SaaS content marketing runs

Four stages, repeated monthly once SaaS content marketing is under way, with the plan adjusted to what the numbers show.

  1. 01

    Research

    Search data, competitors and sales questions gathered into one plan.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Topics prioritised by intent and effort, with owners and dates.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Pages written, reviewed and published with internal links in place.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Results reviewed monthly and the plan adjusted.
