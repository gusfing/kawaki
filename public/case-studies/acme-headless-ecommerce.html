Acme Headless E-commerce

Client Acme Direct
Role UI/UX, Headless Dev
Timeline 12 Weeks
Tech Next.js, Shopify, GSAP

The Challenge

Acme Direct required a modern e-commerce platform upgrade. Their monolithic theme setup introduced performance bottlenecks on mobile devices, limiting user experience. They needed a fast, resilient shopping experience that combined bespoke editorial design with responsive brand storytelling.

Our Approach

We designed a decoupled front-end architecture using Next.js, integrating directly with Shopify's Storefront API. Drawing from our custom web development standards, the architecture features optimized page transitions and interaction design while leveraging Shopify's infrastructure for inventory, customer management, and secure checkout.
