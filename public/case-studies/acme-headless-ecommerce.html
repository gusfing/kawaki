Acme Headless E-commerce

Client Acme Direct
Role UI/UX, Headless Dev
Timeline 12 Weeks
Tech Next.js, Shopify, GSAP

The Challenge

Acme Direct was growing fast, but their monolithic Shopify theme was holding them back. Page load times were averaging 5.8 seconds, severely impacting mobile conversion rates. They needed a lightning-fast shopping experience that didn't compromise on high-end editorial design and interactive brand storytelling.

Our Approach

We decoupled their front-end entirely using Next.js and Vercel, integrating with Shopify's Storefront API. This allowed us to build a completely custom, app-like experience with instant page transitions and rich GSAP animations, while still leveraging Shopify's robust backend for inventory and checkout.

34% Conversion lift
5.8s Load time reduced
<1s New page load
98 Lighthouse Score
