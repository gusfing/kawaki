// 02. PROJECT BRIEF

Strategic Mandate & Operational Objectives

Institutional Context

Urbanland Products LLP is a premier Indian manufacturer of architectural outdoor furniture, public transit shelters, and smart city infrastructure based in Vasai, Maharashtra. The firm manufactures heavy-duty street benches, composite planters, commercial waste bins, bus shelters, gazebos, and modular pre-fab structures engineered from weather-resistant composites and architectural aluminium.

Founded in 2023 by Raj Shekhar, Urbanland serves premier commercial real estate developers (Lodha, Adani, Oberoi), municipal corporations (Mumbai Smart Shelters, Delhi Metro Plazas), and leading landscape architecture practices across India. Their products carry ISO 9001:2015 quality certifications, two-year commercial warranties, and custom fabrication support.

STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE

B2B infrastructure procurement differs fundamentally from retail e-commerce: buyers do not pay via credit card. Instead, landscape architects require exact dimensional drawings, material durability data (WPC vs NFC vs aluminium), and the ability to compile a multi-item specification list for a customized commercial Request for Quote (RFQ).

Verified Project Objectives

OBJ 01

Structure 12 Diverse Infrastructure Domains

Organize hundreds of heavy outdoor infrastructure products (benches, bus shelters, bollards, pergolas) into an intuitive, scalable architectural catalogue.

OBJ 02

Engineer a Multi-Item B2B Request for Quote (RFQ) Cart

Replace standard consumer e-commerce checkout with a commercial quotation engine allowing architects to assemble project schedules and upload architectural drawings.

OBJ 03

Provide Instant Access to Technical CAD Specifications

Enable landscape architects and municipal contractors to inspect structural load ratings, weather-resistance certifications, and download engineering data sheets.

OBJ 04

Dominate B2B Infrastructure Search Visibility

Implement a deep Schema.org graph linking OfferCatalog, LocalBusiness, and FAQPage schemas across all 12 product domains to outrank industrial aggregators.

// 03. THE CHALLENGE & THE APPROACH

Operational Friction vs. Architectural Strategy

THE VERIFIED CHALLENGE

Real Constraints & Friction

Structuring hundreds of heavy industrial products across 12 diverse categories into an intuitive, high-speed catalogue with direct RFP quotation capabilities.

Massive Product Variety & Classification

Organizing 12 distinct product domains (from cast-iron park benches to solar-powered smart bus shelters and modular cabins) without creating an impenetrable navigation maze.

Technical Documentation Requirements

Landscape architects require instant access to CAD drawings, structural load tolerances, and material specifications before including items in municipal tenders.

B2B Quote Request vs Immediate Checkout

Traditional e-commerce shopping carts with retail price tags do not fit commercial bidding, where order volumes vary from 5 luxury resort cabanas to 500 municipal street benches.

Search Visibility & Category SEO

Competing against legacy trade portals (IndiaMART, TradeIndia) required deep semantic search markup across every product classification.

Mobile Usability for Site Engineers

Site supervisors and project managers frequently inspect product specs and assembly details directly on construction sites via mobile smartphones.

THE KAWAKI APPROACH

Architectural Response

We designed Urbanland's digital platform as an engineered technical specification hub. We replaced consumer shopping paradigms with a high-efficiency architectural catalogue that empowers specifiers to find, evaluate, and quote infrastructure components in minutes.

12-Pillar Architectural Information Architecture

We structured the catalogue into 12 definitive domains with standardized product cards featuring material tags, dimensional specs, and installation methods.

B2B Request for Quote (RFQ) Cart

We engineered a multi-item quotation builder allowing architects to assemble project schedules, upload architectural drawings, and request bulk manufacturing bids.

Fullscreen Multi-Column Mega-Menu

To make browsing effortless, we built an interactive fullscreen navigation menu categorizing all 12 product domains with visual thumbnails and quick-jump links.

Deep Schema.org OfferCatalog Graph

We implemented an exhaustive JSON-LD graph linking `OfferCatalog`, `LocalBusiness`, and `FAQPage` schemas to dominate organic search rankings for outdoor infrastructure keywords.
