NursePass

A learning platform that takes internationally trained nurses through professional recognition in Germany.

Visit the live siteStart a project
Client
Healthcare education platform
Built with
Web application
Status
Live

The project

The client

Germany needs many more nurses than it trains, and nurses qualified abroad must pass a recognition process, including a medical German language exam, before they can work. NursePass prepares them for it.

The brief

General course platforms could not handle what recognition requires: clinical scenarios, handover practice, specialist vocabulary and timed exams. NursePass needed a platform built around those steps, in German, that works on a phone between shifts.

What we built

  • A course portal that breaks German healthcare rules and procedures into lessons
  • Medical German practice for patient intake, handovers and documentation
  • Timed practice tests with immediate feedback and scorecards
  • Test progress kept in the browser, so a dropped connection does not lose a session
