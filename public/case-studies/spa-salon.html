Spa & Salon Platform

A booking app and scheduling system for a multi-branch spa and salon business.

Start a project
Client
Booking platform
Built with
Flutter, webhooks

The project

The client

The client runs appointment-based spa, salon and wellness services across several branches.

The brief

Bookings were lost to phone tag, double bookings and idle time between treatments. The business needed customers to book themselves, and a calendar that only offers slots it can really deliver.

What we built

  • A Flutter booking app for choosing services, specialists and packages
  • Scheduling that accounts for specialist shifts, room availability and cleaning time between treatments
  • Service catalogues for each branch
  • Webhook notifications that confirm each appointment straight away
