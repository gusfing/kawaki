Decor Lab

A portfolio website for a Kolkata architecture practice with three decades of work behind it.

The project

The client

Decor Lab was founded in Kolkata in 1993 by Raja Sinha and is led today by Rajdip Sinha, who trained at The Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL. By the firm's own figures, it has completed more than 1,300 projects with a team of 275.

The brief

The practice had moved from traditional architecture into parametric and fluid design. Its website needed to show that new direction without losing three decades of work, and to turn interest from each kind of client into a specific enquiry.

What we built

  • Project galleries with art-directed images for each screen size
  • Material and service catalogues for architecture and interiors
  • An enquiry path for each service the practice offers
  • WebP images and a semantic layout that keep heavy galleries smooth
