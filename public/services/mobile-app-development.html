Which kind of app

The right technology depends on who uses the app and where.

Cross-platform

One codebase for iOS and Android when the app's job is the same on both.

  • Flutter or React Native
  • Booking, ordering and member apps
  • Companion apps for a web platform
  • Faster to launch and maintain

Native

Swift and Kotlin when the app leans on the device: camera, sensors, background work.

  • Swift and SwiftUI for iOS
  • Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android
  • Heavy media or hardware use
  • Platform-specific design

How an app gets built

Test builds reach your phone at every milestone.

  3. 03

    Build

    App and API built in milestones, with test builds on your devices.

  4. 04

    Store release

    Store listings, review submission and the first public release.

  5. 05

    After launch

    Crash reports, updates and new features on a retainer.
