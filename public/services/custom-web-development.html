(04) — Inquiries & FAQ
// 01 What technology stack do you build custom web applications with?
We engineer primarily with Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Node.js for high-performance applications, complemented by headless CMS layers such as Sanity, Strapi, or Shopify Storefront API depending on publishing and transactional requirements.
// 02 How do you achieve sub-second page load times and 95+ Lighthouse scores?
Through disciplined asset management, edge caching on Vercel or Cloudflare, streaming server-rendered HTML, critical CSS extraction, modern AVIF/WebP image pipelines, and strict bundle size budgets.
// 03 Can you migrate our legacy monolithic site to a modern headless architecture?
Yes. We execute zero-downtime headless migrations, preserving all SEO rankings, redirects, customer databases, and product catalogs while replacing the legacy frontend with a lightning-fast custom interface.
// 04 What is the typical timeline for an enterprise custom web development project?
Production-ready editorial flagships and marketing sites typically require 4 to 8 weeks. Complex full-stack web applications with authentication, custom APIs, and third-party integrations generally span 8 to 14 weeks.
