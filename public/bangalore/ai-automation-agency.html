AI automation in Bangalore

AI automation for Bangalore businesses at Kawaki Studios means mapping the work your team repeats by hand, then moving it into workflows that run on their own: triggers, checks, hand-offs and alerts. We build them in n8n or Make.com, connect them to your tools through REST and webhook APIs, and document each one so your team can change it later.

  1. 01

    Mapped before it is built

    Every step, owner and failure case written down before the first node.

  2. 02

    Owned by your team

    Workflows run in your own accounts, with a written guide to each one.

  3. 03

    Checked, not trusted

    Inputs validated, webhooks verified, failures logged and sent to a person.
