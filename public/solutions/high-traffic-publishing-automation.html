What publishing automation for busy websites covers

Publishing automation removes the repetitive steps between a finished draft and a live article: formatting, images, structured data, sitemaps and social posts. We build these workflows for teams that publish often and cannot afford mistakes.

  1. 01

    Fewer manual steps

    Formatting, images and metadata handled by the workflow.

  2. 02

    Checks before publishing

    Missing fields caught before anything goes live.

  3. 03

    Fast pages

    Static pages that stay quick when traffic spikes.

Where publishing slows down

Teams that publish every day lose hours to resizing images, filling metadata and sharing posts by hand. Publishing automation takes those steps over, while an editor still approves every article.

  1. 01

    Draft to live

    Approved drafts published with formatting and images.

  2. 02

    Structured data

    Article JSON-LD, sitemaps and feeds updated.

  3. 03

    Distribution

    Social posts and newsletters queued on publish.

  4. 04

    Static delivery

    Pages built ahead of time so traffic spikes are fine.

How the project runs

Four stages, with every milestone of the publishing automation for busy websites project on a staging link you can open and review.

  1. 01

    Map the workflow

    Every step from draft to promotion.

  2. 02

    Automate

    Workflows in n8n or Make.com, with an approval step.

  3. 03

    Test

    Run on real articles before switching over.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Documentation and a walkthrough for editors.
