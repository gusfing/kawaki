What website blacklist removal covers

Website blacklist removal at Kawaki Studios follows one rule: find out how the attacker got in before calling the site clean. We remove malicious code, backdoors and spam pages, close the entry point, restore what was damaged, and leave you with backups and monitoring so a repeat is caught early.

  1. 01

    Entry point first

    Cleanup is not finished until we know how the attacker got in.

  2. 02

    Nothing left behind

    Files, database, users and scheduled tasks checked, not just visible pages.

  3. 03

    Backups you can trust

    Stored off the server and restored on purpose, not hoped for.

Signs you need website blacklist removal

Visitors redirected to spam, Google listing pages in Japanese or pharmacy terms, a hosting suspension or a browser warning are the usual signs. Website blacklist removal deals with the visible damage and with the access that caused it, in that order.

  1. 01

    Malware and backdoor removal

    Infected files, injected scripts and hidden admin users removed.

  2. 02

    Spam and redirect cleanup

    Spam pages removed from search and redirects traced to their source.

  3. 03

    Blacklist and warning removal

    Review requests filed once the site is verifiably clean.

  4. 04

    Hardening and backups

    Updates, permissions, firewall rules and tested off-site backups.

How website blacklist removal works

Four stages, from containing the damage to a written report on what happened and what changed during website blacklist removal.

  1. 01

    Contain

    Site isolated or put into maintenance, with a full copy kept first.

  2. 02

    Clean

    Malicious code, users and spam removed from files and database.

  3. 03

    Close

    Entry point patched, passwords rotated, hardening applied.

  4. 04

    Report

    What was found, what was done and what to watch.
