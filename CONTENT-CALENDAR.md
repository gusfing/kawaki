## Content Rules

- Min lengths: guides 1,200w · comparisons 1,500w · case studies 1,000w · research 2,500w
- Every post: named author bio w/ credentials, FAQ section (FAQPage schema), TOC, one CTA block
- Case studies need real metrics before publishing; placeholders stay draft
- Repurpose: each guide → LinkedIn carousel + newsletter issue
