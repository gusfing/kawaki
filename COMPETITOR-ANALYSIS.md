# Kawaki Studios — Competitive Analysis

**Method:** Qualitative SERP + site review of boutique web dev studios ranking for service terms. No live URL for Kawaki yet, so gap analysis is directional. DataForSEO tools not connected; estimates noted as such.

| # | Competitor | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses / Gap |
|---|-----------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | **DONE HQ** (donehq.com) | Boutique webdev partner for marketing leaders; headless WordPress/Webflow/React/Sanity | Metric-rich case studies (45% load cut, 100K daily readers), embedded-partner narrative, LinkedIn content engine | No industries hub, blog thin on evergreen guides |

| 4 | **cre8** (cre8.agency) | B2B/regulated-industry web dev, PHP/.NET/Craft | 25+ yrs authority, enterprise logos (JLL), migration case studies | Dated design, weak GEO/AI-readiness, generic service copy |
| 5 | **Tunel Studio** (tunel.studio) | Full-scope digital presence from scratch, multilingual | Documented SEO results (65% organic lift, 10% CTR), from-zero launch playbook | Younger domain, thinner service depth |

## What Winners Do (Patterns)

1. **Case studies carry specific numbers** — every top competitor leads with quantified outcomes. Kawaki's current case studies are placeholder-named (Acme, Curology) with no metrics → biggest gap.
2. **Named methodology** — "Web Blueprint", "embedded partner model". Kawaki has "Editorial Engineering" — underused as a searchable concept.
3. **Service pages target long-tail** — "headless commerce development agency" style terms, not "web development".
4. **E-E-A-T surfaces**: team bios, client logos, testimonials, Clutch/G2 profiles.
5. **Schema present but inconsistent** across all five — opportunity to out-execute.

## E-E-A-T Benchmark

- All five show team faces/bios; only DONE HQ + Commence link social profiles via schema
- Client logos: cre8 strongest; Kawaki needs permission-based logo wall
- Reviews: cre8 cites Clutch — Kawaki should build Clutch profile early (DR-passing link + trust signal)

## Domain Authority Estimates *(directional, unverified)*
