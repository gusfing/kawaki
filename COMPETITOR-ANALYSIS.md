**Website:** netguru.com  
**DA:** 68 | **Organic Keywords:** 12,400+ | **Est. Traffic:** 45,000+/mo  
**HQ:** Poznań, Poland (Global, strong India presence)

- Massive content library (2,000+ blog posts, guides, case studies)

## Conclusion

**Kawaki's Winning Position:** "Editorial Engineering" — the only studio combining Swiss editorial design systems with hardened Next.js performance architecture, delivered by senior engineers directly, with SLA-backed permanence.

**Primary Competitive Moats to Build:**
1. **Term Ownership** — "Editorial Engineering" as branded methodology
2. **Technical Depth** — Production-grade headless commerce, WCAG in CI, 0ms CLS
3. **Trust Signals** — Direct engineer access, SLA support, 100+ deployments
4. **Content Authority** — Original research, open source, technical guides competitors won't write

**Biggest Threat:** Netguru's content volume and authority. **Counter:** Niche depth over breadth, own the "editorial engineering" term, superior technical implementation as proof.
