# The Brief
We re-platformed Acme Corp's legacy storefront onto Shopify's headless Hydrogen framework, allowing their team complete layout control and fast page loads.

# The Challenge
Their standard Shopify theme was weighed down by excessive third-party apps, causing slow page transitions, cart bottlenecks, and high drop-offs during peak traffic events.

# The Solution
We migrated their site to Hydrogen & Oxygen, decoupling the front-end rendering from back-end logic. We coded custom Liquid templates and Shopify Flow automation scripts directly.
