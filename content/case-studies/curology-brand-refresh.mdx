# The Brief
Curology needed a comprehensive brand refresh to align with their modern values and appeal to a younger audience. We rebuilt their layout files, refined messaging, and optimized page speed metrics.

# The Challenge
Their previous website relied heavily on visual builders that created excessive DOM elements and slowed loading speeds on mobile devices. The visual identity also lacked consistent typography and modern alignment layouts.

# The Solution
We engineered a clean Next.js application, translating Figma design specs into custom responsive layouts. We optimized image assets, implemented fluid micro-interactions, and established a modular, lightweight component architecture.
