# Scoping the right fit
When you select an agency, you're not just buying code — you're choosing a technical collaborator who will impact your search rankings, conversion rates, and long-term operational velocity.

# Red flags to watch out for
1. Over-reliance on third-party page builders that create bloated code.
2. Lack of direct access to the engineering team.
3. Proprietary CMS systems that lock you in.

# The right questions to ask
1. Do you write semantic, accessible HTML?
2. What are your performance SLAs and Core Web Vitals guarantees?
3. How do you handle site updates and dependency patches post-launch?
