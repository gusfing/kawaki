A static brochure site does little to engage users. Most B2B brands fall into the trap of using identical stock templates with generic lists of services and a simple contact button. They miss opportunities to offer immediate value.

By integrating interactive elements—such as ROI calculators, custom project estimators, or solution-oriented quizzes—you empower potential clients to find answers immediately. This interactive friction builds trust.

For a fintech partner, we built a custom ROI calculator that allowed users to input transaction volume and see potential savings instantly. This single interactive feature raised marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) by 45%.

Interactive features turn a passive browsing experience into an active conversation, making your brand's value proposition tangible and immediate.
