Why This Works

● Unexpected

Dark indigo + red + yellow is bold and distinctive. Not the generic dark + neon trap. This combination signals premium and precision.

● Premium Feel

Deep indigo evokes luxury. Red is confident and energetic. Yellow adds warmth without being playful. Together: high-end and human.

● High Contrast

Red on indigo? Sharp. Yellow on indigo? Sophisticated. Text remains readable. Accessibility standards met with style.

● Founder Psychology

Red says: "We're bold. We execute." Yellow says: "We're human. We listen." Indigo says: "We're sophisticated. Trust us."

● Memorable

Your founder remembers the indigo-red combo. It sticks. It's defensible. It's yours. Not one of a thousand dark + neon sites.

● Converts

Red CTAs pull eyes. Yellow accents guide attention. Indigo grounds the experience. Psychology + design = conversions.
