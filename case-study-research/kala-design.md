## 1. Project Summary & Background
Kala Design Co. is a contemporary interior design and architecture agency operating across residential and commercial sectors. The studio emphasizes timeless elegance, materiality, and emotional resonance in built environments ("bridging the gap between architecture, interiors, and emotion"). The digital experience requires an editorial, high-craft presentation that reflects their bespoke spatial design standards, showcasing luxury residences, corporate spaces, and turnkey interior commissions.

## 7. Recommended Case Study Narrative
1. **The Objective:** Translate an editorial interior design philosophy into a digital flagship that conveys restraint, physical texture, and spatial authority without generic agency design templates.
2. **Design & Editorial Execution:** Custom newspaper-inspired masthead layout, bespoke typography pairings (editorial serif + mono accents), full-screen project photography, and micro-interactions.
3. **Information Architecture:** Seamless categorization across Luxury Residential, Commercial, and Turnkey Execution, driving high-intent inquiries to direct studio contact channels.
