## 1. Project Summary & Background
Urbanland Products is a leading Indian manufacturer of architectural outdoor furniture, public transit shelters, and smart city infrastructure. Serving commercial developers, luxury hospitality resorts, municipal corporations, and landscape architects across India, Urbanland manufactures heavy-duty benches, planters, pergolas, bus shelters, and modular structures built from weather-resistant composites and architectural aluminium.

The digital requirement is distinct from typical retail e-commerce: rather than immediate consumer checkout, the platform requires a complex, multi-category technical product catalogue, architectural specification downloads, material durability guides, and high-conversion B2B Request-for-Proposal (RFP) quotation workflows.

## 7. Recommended Case Study Narrative
1. **The B2B Challenge:** B2B industrial procurement involves multiple stakeholders—architects checking dimensions, contractors reviewing structural loads, and procurement heads negotiating bulk volume. The site needed to serve all three without turning into a confusing PDF dump.
2. **Information Architecture & Mega-Menu:** Engineered a hierarchical 12-pillar product catalog with real-time category filtering, architectural CAD/specification download access, and responsive mobile navigation.
3. **Quote-Driven Commerce:** Replaced traditional shopping cart checkout with an enterprise RFP workflow, allowing project managers to request milestone quotes based on project drawings and volume tiers.
