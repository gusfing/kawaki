## 1. Project Summary & Background
Decor Lab is a premier architecture, interior design, and landscape practice established in 1993 with over 33 years of institutional heritage across India. Headquartered in Kolkata with over 1,300 completed sites and 275+ team members, the firm bridges traditional craftsmanship with computational parametric design under the leadership of Rajdip Sinha (trained at the Bartlett, UCL). 

Kawaki Studios engineered Decor Lab's high-concept digital flagship using Next.js, featuring ambient video backgrounds, horizontal scroll mechanics, custom viewport cursor interactions, and an interactive spatial project gallery.

## 3. Verified Business & Product Facts (Official Domain Evidence)
* **Heritage & Founders:** Founded in 1993 by Raja Sinha; legacy carried forward by Rajdip Sinha (The Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL).
* **Practice Scale:** 33 years in continuous practice, 275-strong team, projects across 15 Indian cities, and 1,300+ completed residential, institutional, and commercial sites.

* **Design Approach Pillars:**

## 7. Recommended Case Study Narrative
1. **The Architectural Challenge:** Translating three decades of architectural heritage and Bartlett-trained parametric computational modeling into a digital presence that feels physically grounded yet technically futuristic.
2. **Next.js Engineering Solution:** Engineered a full-screen sensory web experience on Next.js combining ambient cinematic video backgrounds, a horizontal spatial gallery, and fluid micro-interactions without performance bottlenecks.
